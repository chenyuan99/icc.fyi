Microsoft · Voluntary Exit Offer

Microsoft announced a voluntary, time-limited exit offer for eligible employees in participating organizations, with financial support and career planning resources.

Announced October 26, 2026 · Source: Amy Coleman, Microsoft Chief People Officer

Who is eligible?

  • Employees at level 70 and below in participating teams.
  • Canada, India, the United Kingdom and the United States.
  • Availability varies by organization; the offer is not company-wide.

Where to find your details

Microsoft said eligible employees would receive an email linking to their individual offer details.

The public announcement does not specify payout amounts or an application deadline.

Read the official announcement

Microsoft names this program the Voluntary Exit Offer (VEO). See the original post for the full announcement.

Google · Voluntary buyouts

Reported November 4, 2026 · Source: Reuters

Google confirmed to Reuters that it expanded voluntary exit packages in June 2026 to some employees in Canada, France, Germany, Japan and Switzerland.

Coverage and timing

The expansion follows programs in some US and UK divisions in 2025. Reuters could not establish how many employees accepted the latest offers.

This report describes earlier offers; it does not establish a current application window.

Switzerland: reported terms

People familiar with the program told Reuters it covered multiple divisions but excluded DeepMind. Accepted employees received one month’s salary per year of service, capped at 12 months.

These reported terms apply to Switzerland and should not be assumed for other countries.

Google · US Platforms and Devices (2025)

Reported January 30, 2025 · Source: The Verge · Historical report

The Verge reported that Google offered US employees in its Platforms and Devices organization, including Android and Pixel teams, a voluntary departure program with severance. This was a division-specific offer, separate from the later international expansion.
