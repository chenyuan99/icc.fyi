Microsoft Voluntary Exit Offer

Microsoft announced a voluntary, time-limited exit offer for eligible employees in participating organizations, with financial support and career planning resources.

Announced October 26, 2026 · Source: Amy Coleman, Microsoft Chief People Officer

Who is eligible?

  • Employees at level 70 and below in participating teams.
  • Canada, India, the United Kingdom and the United States.
  • Availability varies by organization; the offer is not company-wide.

Where to find your details

Microsoft said eligible employees would receive an email linking to their individual offer details.

The public announcement does not specify payout amounts or an application deadline.

Read the official announcement

Microsoft names this program the Voluntary Exit Offer (VEO). See the original post for the full announcement.
